Apple Slab Pie

  • Home

Description

Apple slab pie is a delicious dessert that combines the flavors of fresh apples with a buttery crust. This recipe will guide you through the steps to make a homemade apple slab pie that is perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 4 1/2 sticks(18 ounces) cold unsalted butter, divided
  • 5 1/2 cupsall-purpose flour, divided, plus more for rolling
  • 3 teaspoonsgranulated sugar, divided
  • 2 tablespoons kosher salt,divided
  • 1/4 cup cold water,divided

Steps

  1. Make the pie dough.Adapted from The Kitchn’s Flaky Butter Pie Crust, this dough comes together quickly, rolls out beautifully, and bakes to crisp, buttery, deeply golden perfection.
  2. Make the filling.Combine the diced apples with the dry ingredients, stirring to coat the fruit completely.
  3. Roll out the dough.Let the pie doughs sit out at room temperature for 10 minutes before rolling to help prevent cracking. Dust a rolling pin with flour and roll the first dough out on a work surface generously dusted with flour, then carefully transfer to a 13×18-inch baking sheet. Evenly sprinkle with a combination of flour and sugar, which helps seal the bottom crust from the juicy filling and gives you a crisp, golden-brown result.
  4. Assemble the pie.Transfer the filling to the prepared bottom crust, leaving a border at the edges. Dot with cold butter and refrigerate while you roll out the second dough, then gently top the filling with the remaining crust. Seal, then crimp the edges. Refrigerate the pie for 30 minutes while the oven preheats.
  5. Bake and cool the pie.Brush the top and edges of the pie with egg wash and sprinkle with turbinado sugar. Bake in the lower third of the oven for the best possible browning on both the top and bottom crusts. I know it’s nearly impossible to resist warm apple pie, but let the pie cool completely so that the filling sets up properly. (You can reheat the pie before serving if you’d like.)
  6. Make the glaze.Whisk together powdered sugar, heavy cream, and vanilla bean paste to make a simple, rich glaze. Drizzle it over the pie before slicing and serving.
